Happy Birthday, My Special Soul Dhanu 🎂

En chella friend Dhanu… 💗 Innaikku un birthday. Unakku wish panna neraya vaarthaigal irukku, aana edhai mudhal-la sollanum-nu enakku theriyala. Namma ippo munna maadhiri pesala-naalum, oru kaalathula namma share pannina memories enakku mukkiyam dhaan. Adhellam nadakkave illa-nu ennaala solla mudiyadhu. Nee en life-la oru azhagana part-ah irundhirukka; adhai naan marandhutta maadhiri nadikka virumbala. Namma sirichadhu, pesinadhu, chinna chinna vishayathukkellaam sandhoshappattadhu, sila neram sanda pottadhu… ellame namma kadhaiyoda oru part. Sila memories ninaikkumbodhu sirippu varum; sila memories konjam valikkavum seiyum. Aana andha azhagana moments-um irundhuchu-nu naan eppovume namburen. Innaikku un birthday-la naan manasaara wish panradhu ore vishayam dhaan: nee nalla irukkanum, Dhanu. Un manasu light-ah irukkanum. Unna purinjukkura people un kooda irukkanum. Nee aasai padra dreams ellam unakku sariyana nerathula kidaikkanum. Un smile unmaiyaana sandhoshathoda irukkanum. Unakku kashtamaana naal vandhaalum, adhu eppovume appadiye irukkaadhu-nu ninaichukko. Unna nee kuraichu ninaikkaadha. Nee neraya azhagana vishayangalukku deserve panra. Un dreams-ai vidaadha; un heart-ai kindness-oda vechukko. Namma friendship eppadi maarinaalum, indha wish unakku oru chinna smile kuduthaa, indha surprise create pannadhu worth dhaan. Idhu endha expectation-um illaama, un birthday special-ah irukkanum-nu manasula irundhu create pannadhu. Happy Birthday, Dhanu. Un life-la innum neraya happiness, peace, love, beautiful beginnings varanum. Eppovume nalla iru. 🦋💗
